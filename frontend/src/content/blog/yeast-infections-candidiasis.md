# **Yeast Infections (Candidiasis): The Sugar-Fungus Connection**

**You treat it. It goes away. Two weeks later, the itch is back.**

You are clean. You shower twice a day. You even use intimate wash (which we need to talk about later). So why does it feel like there is a permanent fire "down there"?

If you are stuck in a loop of buying antifungal creams every month, the problem might not be your hygiene. The problem might be your **blood sugar**.

## **What This Article Helps You Understand**

* The science behind Yeast infection  
* Why sugar and blood glucose matter more than most people realise  
* When to investigate further instead of repeating treatments



### **The Science: The "Bakery" Inside You**

Here is the truth: You naturally have yeast (*Candida albicans*) living in your vagina. It’s supposed to be there, living peacefully alongside good bacteria.

But yeast has a weakness: **It loves sugar.**

When you eat a lot of sugar or refined carbs, your blood glucose spikes. This glucose leaks into your vaginal secretions, creating a "buffet" for the yeast. Fed by this sugar, the yeast multiplies rapidly, overpowering the good bacteria and causing the thick, white discharge and intense itching we know as Candidiasis.



### **The Clinical Insight: Sugar-Fungus Connection** 

Recurrent yeast infections (more than 4 times a year) are often the **first silent sign of Insulin Resistance or Prediabetes**.

If your body is struggling to process insulin (very common in Indian women with PMOS), your blood sugar remains slightly elevatednot high enough to be "Diabetes," but high enough to feed the fungus.

* **The Cycle:** You eat high carbs \> Blood sugar rises \> Yeast overgrows \> You use cream \> Yeast dies down \> You eat high carbs \> Yeast returns.  
* **The Clue:** If you get infections right before your period (when cravings spike) or after a festive week of eating sweets, the trigger is likely systemic, not local.



## **Common Symptoms**

* Intense itching (often worse at night)  
* Thick white discharge (usually odorless)  
* Redness, soreness, or burning  
* Pain during intercourse  
* External irritation more than internal pain

**Note**  
Recurrent yeast infections often itch **more** than they hurt.



## **When to Check Blood Sugar**

You should consider testing if:

* Yeast infections keep returning  
* You have PMOS or irregular cycles  
* You crave sugar or feel energy crashes  
* You have a family history of diabetes  
* Weight gain is unexplained

Simple tests often used:

* Fasting blood sugar  
* HbA1c (3-month average)

Even “borderline” numbers matter.

### **The "Dettol" Mistake**

We have a cultural obsession with being "clean." When we feel an itch, our instinct is to scrub harder.

* **The Myth:** "Itching means I am dirty."  
* **The Reality:** Many Indian women add **Dettol or Savlon** to their bathwater or use harsh soaps to "sanitise" the area. **STOP immediately.**  
  * These antiseptics kill the *good* bacteria (Lactobacillus) that fight off the yeast. By "cleaning" too much, you are actually removing your body's natural defence system, making it easier for the yeast to take over.



### **The Fix: Starve the Yeast**

**1\. Cutting Back on Added Sugar** For 2 weeks, cut out **all** added sugar. Yes, even the sugar in your *chai*. High blood sugar creates conditions yeast thrives in, so this is worth trying alongside (not instead of) proper treatment. If your symptoms ease, mention it to your doctor; if they persist or keep coming back, get assessed rather than continuing to self-test through diet, since recurrent infections can point to diabetes or another underlying cause.

**2\. Probiotics: The Reinforcements** You need to rebuild the army of good bacteria.

* **Eat:** A bowl of homemade *dahi* (curd) every day.  
* **Supplement:** Look for a probiotic capsule specifically containing *Lactobacillus rhamnosus* and *Lactobacillus reuteri*. These strains are proven to reach the vaginal microbiome.

**3\. Wear Breathable Fabrics** Yeast loves warm, moist environments. The humid Indian weather \+ synthetic underwear \= disaster.

* **Rule:** 100% Cotton underwear only. And at night? Go without. Let the skin breathe.



## **Dr. Rove’s Note**

"If you have treated a yeast infection 3 times in 6 months and it keeps coming back, put down the cream and pick up a lab form. Get your **HbA1c (average blood sugar)** and **Fasting Blood Sugar** checked. You are likely treating the symptom (the itch) while ignoring the root cause (the sugar)."
